## What I do, in plain words

- **Show what your marketing really brings in.** I connect your website, ads, shop and CRM data so you see which campaigns make money, without breaking privacy rules (GDPR).
- **Make data reliable.** Every number is checked automatically, every report can be rebuilt, and nothing breaks silently.
- **Automate the boring and the risky.** From weekly reports to trading systems with hard safety limits, the rules live in code, not in someone's head.

This is also what [Vollcom Digital](https://github.com/VollcomDigital) does: **digital analytics, tracking, ad tech, and data & AI strategy on the cloud** for companies that want to use their own data better.

## For investors and partners

Next to client work I build **systematic trading**: software that trades by fixed rules instead of gut feeling.

- **Safety first:** every strategy goes from backtest to paper trading to real money, never straight to live.
- **Hard limits in code:** automatic stop when losses reach a set limit, plus position limits, and a person must restart it.
- **Honest results:** the track record is checked and timestamped so it can't be polished afterwards ([how](./docs/verified-track-record.md)).

## How I work

1. **Same input, same result:** re-running a job never changes the numbers.
2. **No peeking into the future:** analyses only use data that was available at the time.
3. **Limits are code:** budgets and risk limits are enforced automatically.
4. **Tested before it ships:** automated checks run on every change.
